How NDVI works

☀️ Sunlight contains visible light and near-infrared (NIR) light. A camera on a drone or satellite measures how much of each bounces back.

🌿 Healthy chlorophyll absorbs red light (used for photosynthesis) and the leaf's cell structure reflects NIR strongly (to avoid overheating). We see green; the sensor sees a huge NIR "glow".

🥀 A stressed or dying plant has less chlorophyll (reflects more red) and a collapsing leaf structure (reflects less NIR). The gap shrinks.

NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)
  • 🟢 0.6 – 1.0 dense, healthy vegetation
  • 🟡 0.2 – 0.5 stressed or sparse plants
  • 🟤 −0.1 – 0.1 bare soil, dead plants
  • 🔴 below 0 water, wet mud
